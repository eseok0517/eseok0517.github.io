Method and assumptions

  • Net book value uses straight-line depreciation with zero salvage value: cost × (1 − age / useful life), floored at 0. Rows with no useful life use the default in Settings.
  • Age uses acquisition date to today for uploads; public Seattle mode uses in-service date to November 8, 2026. Unsupported public-source metrics are unavailable.
  • Audited in last 12 months counts assets whose last audit date falls within the past 365 days.
  • Maintenance cost ratio is total annual maintenance cost divided by total acquisition cost.
  • Data completeness is the share of filled cells across the columns you mapped.
  • Conditions are normalized to Good / Fair / Poor; anything unrecognized becomes Unknown.
