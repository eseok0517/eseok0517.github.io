How much inventory is worth holding?

Manufacturing Purchasing Strategy

Compare replenishment frequency, supplier discounts, and inventory investment against a 90-day ordering policy.

Finding

Frequent ordering reduces inventory investment, but its lower unit fill rate creates a service tradeoff.

Recommendation: Evaluate frequent ordering for noncritical supplies; review shortage costs before changing critical items.

Do market regimes change forward returns?

Market Regimes and Forward Returns

A supporting economics project: align four public data series, classify market regimes, and compare subsequent 12-month price returns.

Finding & interpretation

Regime averages vary across the historical sample. Overlapping return windows and different sample lengths limit the conclusions.

Use: Historical research and scenario context. The analysis does not establish a reliable trading rule.
