How checkout changed

From chat messages to scanned records.

The same four stages, before and after the barcode system.

1Pickup2Record3Monitor4Restock
BeforeManual, written records
Pickup

Operator takes the item

Depends on the operator remembering to report it
Record

Written message left in a third-party chat app

Sensitive details sit outside company security controls
Monitor

Coordinators read back through written chat logs every so often

Written records, not searchable data; gaps go unnoticed
Restock

Restock when manual checks seem low

Stock judged by eye; easy to miscount or miss
AfterBarcode scanning
Pickup

Operator scans the item on the way out

Scan replaces manual reporting
Record

Logged automatically to company records

Data stays inside company systems
Monitor

Spreadsheet flags items running low

Low stock is flagged, not guessed
Restock

Coordinators work from the restock list

Starts from recorded data
