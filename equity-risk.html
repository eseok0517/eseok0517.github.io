Equity risk and regimes

This dashboard covers ten widely held stocks and ETFs, including two banks. It compares return against risk, shows how deep each fell in past drawdowns, tests how each performed in the year after an inverted yield curve or high inflation, and lets you build a simple portfolio from them.

Method and limits

  • Data. Month-end prices for SPY, QQQ, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, JPM, GS, XOM, JNJ and TLT, refreshed weekly by a GitHub Action. Yield curve (10-year minus 2-year Treasury), CPI and the 3-month bill rate come from FRED. The file notes which price source was used for each security.
  • Regimes. The curve is inverted when the 10Y–2Y spread is below zero at month-end. Inflation is high when CPI is up at least 3% from a year earlier. Each month is labeled by its regime, then the following 12-month price return is averaged.
  • Overlap. Forward windows overlap, so neighboring months are not independent. The number of separate inversion episodes is shown with the results, and it is small.
  • Not included. Taxes, trading costs, and survivorship: these are securities that are well known today. Past results do not predict future returns.
