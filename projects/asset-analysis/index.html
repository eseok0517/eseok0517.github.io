Interpretation

What needs follow-up, and why?

Start with data quality

A missing field can be a publication limitation. Repeated IDs or zero recorded cost warrant source review; neither proves that equipment is missing or needs replacement.

Then review operating risk

Use recorded in-service dates and planned replacement years to ask planning questions. Condition, maintenance spending, and operational criticality would be needed to justify replacement.

Recommendation: Check cost exceptions and validate planned replacement years with the responsible department. Obtain maintenance and condition records before recommending capital expenditure.

Method and interpretation

Metric definitions and limits.

Public Seattle mode leaves book value, useful-life, condition, maintenance, and audit KPIs unavailable because the source does not support them. The definitions below apply to uploaded registers or the explicitly synthetic demo when those fields exist.

MetricCalculation or treatmentWhat it supports
Estimated net book valueStraight-line depreciation; zero salvage value; floor at zero. Missing useful life uses the editable default.A simplified accounting estimate, not market value or replacement priority.

Implementation

How the tool works.

Import and map

Accept a CSV or Excel register, suggest column matches, and let users correct the mapping. Asset ID and cost are required fields.

Calculate and export

Normalize fields, apply editable assumptions and targets, calculate 10 KPIs, then export cleaned records or the exceptions list.
