02 / Management interpretation

What the records support.

01

Validate apparent concentration

Large supplier-name groups identify where to investigate dependency, contract coverage, and procurement classification. Concentration alone does not establish excessive pricing.

02

Resolve exceptions before comparisons

Investigate repeated lines, missing dates, nonpositive amounts, and quantity × unit price differences. Taxes, fees, amendments, and legitimate repeated purchases can explain apparent exceptions.

03

Match specifications first

The actual downloaded extract has no unit-of-measure field or reliable common SKU. Generic descriptions and quantities cannot establish comparable unit prices or volume discounts.

Sizing the follow-up: The 862 flagged lines carry $5.9M in recorded positive amounts, 2.7% of the $217.7M selected spend, so verification starts with a small share of spend. Recommended next step: Verify source documents for flagged records and classify comparable items before negotiating or estimating savings. These are public-sector records, not company records.

03 / Evidence & method

Sources and limits.

Defined selection

Read all 344,504 original rows. Retain all 14,823 lines in three exact Segment Title categories: Manufacturing Components and Supplies; Tools and General Machinery; Electrical Systems and Lighting and Components and Accessories and Supplies. No keyword cherry-picking or automatic deduplication.

Preserved transaction context

Keep source row, PO, department, supplier identifiers, item descriptions, quantities, and amounts. A department/PO pair is a descriptive grouping, not a unique line key. Identical selected business fields create a repeated-line review candidate.

Dates and amount checks

Parse dates and monetary values. Dates before 2000 or after the snapshot date are flagged and excluded from date grouping; valid creation date is the fallback for missing purchase date. Flag extended-amount differences over $0.05. Positive line totals form the descriptive amount measure; nonpositive values remain in the export.

What cannot be inferred

No receipt, invoice payment, stock balance, consumption history, supplier lead time, or physical audit result is supplied. Consequently this case makes no claim about actual savings, stockouts, fill rates, or optimized order quantities. The source's historical records and classification quality limit generalization.
